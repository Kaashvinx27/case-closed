FIELD MANUAL STANDARD OPERATIVE PROCEDURE

Bureau Investigation Protocol

This manual outlines the systematic investigative methodology required to observe crime scenes, expose alibi falsehoods, and construct logically airtight murder warrants.

The Ten Rules of Detective Deduction

01. Select an Open Case Dossier

Browse the repository and choose an active case file matched to your difficulty preference.

02. Study the Briefing & Timeline

Note down the incident time, victim background, and initial coroner findings.

03. Search Crime Scene Coordinates

Inspect glowing coordinate markers on the crime scene map to catalog physical objects and documents.

04. Analyze Forensic Notes

Read chemical toxicology, ballistics, and document provenance in the evidence detail modal.

05. Question Every Suspect

Interview all persons of interest regarding their stated alibis, motives, and whereabouts.

06. Establish Contradictions

When a suspect's claim conflicts directly with cataloged evidence, confront them to establish a formal contradiction.

07. Connect Corkboard Red Strings

Link clues and suspects on the corkboard canvas to visually establish relationships and timeline leaks.

08. Consult Bureau Hints Prudently

First hints are free; deeper hints deduct points from your final evaluation score.

09. Submit Final Accusation Warrant

Select the perpetrator, specify their underlying motive, check supporting evidence, and detail deduction reasoning.

10. Review Outcome & Epilogue

The backend validates your warrant against the sealed solution and calculates your official score and detective rank.

Case Study: Anatomy of a Contradiction

Hypothetical Scenario: Suspect claims: "I never touched the study safe and spent the evening watching television in the library from 10:00 to 11:30 PM."

Discovered Evidence Clue: Library power logbook reveals the television circuit breaker tripped at 10:05 PM and remained unpowered until morning.

Contradiction Deduction: Confronting the suspect with the breaker log proves the alibi is fabricated, unlocking admissions of unauthorized presence near the safe.
